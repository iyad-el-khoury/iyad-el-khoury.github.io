### ÉNS Lyon Second Concours
L'École Normale Supérieure de Lyon is a research oriented institution in France, both in the theoretical sciences as well as literature, economics, geography and history. As a highly selective program, the school has different admission contests, each targeting a specific profile. In my case, as a student enrolled in a scientific bachelor program, I had to pass the so called **[second concours](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/formation/admission/procedures-dadmission/second-concours)**, which consists of a first selection via a written exam and a final selection via oral exams. 

To give an idea of what is expected, here are the past **[exams](https://banques-ecoles.fr/cms/filieres-universitaires-second-concours-scientifique/les-annales-et-rapports-des-jurys-2/#mathematiques)**. I myself passed the mathematics and computer science tests.

As part of the oral exams, every student must write a rapport on a topic of their choice. Naturally, I chose to talk about my own research interests in combinatorics, more specifically on the problem of constructing explicit Ramsey graphs.
