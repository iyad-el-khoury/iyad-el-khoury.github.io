Let $$(V, \lVert \cdot \rVert)$$ be a normed real vector space and $$v_1, v_2, \dots, v_n \in V$$ a family of $$n$$ unit vectors in $$V$$. A famous problem in geometry is to ask how small can the vector $$\varepsilon_1 v_1 + \dots + \varepsilon_n v_n$$ be made, where $$\varepsilon_i \in \{-1,1\}$$ is a choice of sign for each vector $$v_i$$. Our task is thus to find some upperbound $$f(n)$$ such that for an arbitrary family of $$n$$ unit vectors there exists some choice of signs $$\{\varepsilon_i\}_{i} \in \{-1,1\}$$ such that $$\lVert\varepsilon_1 v_1 + \dots + \varepsilon_n v_n\rVert \leq f(n)$$
