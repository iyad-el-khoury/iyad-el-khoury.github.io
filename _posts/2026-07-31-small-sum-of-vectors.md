Let $$(V, || ||)$$ be a normed vector space and $$v_1, v_2, \dots, v_n \in V$$ some vectors in $$V$$. A famous problem in geometry is to ask how small can we make the vector $$ \epsilon_1 v_1 + \dots + \epsilon_n v_n $$, where $$\epsilon_1, \dots,\epsilon_n \in \{-1,1\}$$ is a choice of sign for each vector $$v_i$$.
